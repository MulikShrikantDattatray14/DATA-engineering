# 26. 2024 revenue, month by month, running

## Question and image

Finance wants the 2024 story: each month's revenue and the cumulative revenue for the year so far.

Using the `Invoice` table and only 2024 invoices, return exactly three columns:

- `Month` — formatted as `YYYY-MM`
- `Revenue` — that month's total, rounded to 2 decimal places
- `CumulativeRevenue` — the year-to-date running total through that month, rounded to 2 decimal places

Sort by `Month` ascending.

Expected output: `Month`, `Revenue`, `CumulativeRevenue` — 12 rows; order matters.
