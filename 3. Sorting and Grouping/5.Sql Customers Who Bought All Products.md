# 5. SQL Customers Who Bought All Products

## Question and image

Given the `Customer` and `Product` tables, report every customer who bought all products listed in the `Product` table.

The `Customer` table may contain duplicate rows. A customer qualifies only when their distinct purchased products cover every product in `Product`.

Return exactly one column:

- `customer_id`

Sort the result by `customer_id` ascending.

### Example

**Input — Customer**

| customer_id | product_key |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 5 |
| 3 | 6 |
| 1 | 6 |

**Input — Product**

| product_key |
|---:|
| 5 |
| 6 |

**Output**

| customer_id |
|---:|
| 1 |
| 3 |

Customers 1 and 3 bought both available products, 5 and 6.

## Schema

**Customer**  
customer_id, product_key

- `customer_id` — integer; not `NULL`
- `product_key` — integer; foreign key referencing `Product.product_key`
- The table may contain duplicate rows.

**Product**  
product_key

- `product_key` — integer; primary key
