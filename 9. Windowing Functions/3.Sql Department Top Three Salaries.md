# 3. SQL Department Top Three Salaries

## Question and image

[**185. Department Top Three Salaries**](https://leetcode.com/problems/department-top-three-salaries/)

Find the employees whose salaries are among the **three highest distinct salary values** in their department. Include every employee tied at a qualifying salary.

Return `Department`, `Employee`, and `Salary` in any order.

**Example 1 — input:**

Employee:

| id | name | salary | departmentId |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Joe | 85000 | 1 |
| 2 | Henry | 80000 | 2 |
| 3 | Sam | 60000 | 2 |
| 4 | Max | 90000 | 1 |
| 5 | Janet | 69000 | 1 |
| 6 | Randy | 85000 | 1 |
| 7 | Will | 70000 | 1 |

Department:

| id | name |
|---:|---|
| 1 | IT |
| 2 | Sales |

**Output (one valid order):**

| Department | Employee | Salary |
|---|---|---:|
| IT | Max | 90000 |
| IT | Joe | 85000 |
| IT | Randy | 85000 |
| IT | Will | 70000 |
| Sales | Henry | 80000 |
| Sales | Sam | 60000 |

**Explanation:** IT's top three distinct salary values are 90,000, 85,000, and 70,000. Joe and Randy both qualify at 85,000. Sales has only two salary values, so both employees qualify.
